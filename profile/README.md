# KIT-IWU Hydraulic Engineering and Water Resources Management

Welcome to the GitHub page of the **Hydraulic Engineering and Water Resources Management** group at the [Institute for Water and Environment (IWU)](https://www.iwu.kit.edu/wb/index.php), [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT)](https://www.kit.edu).

Our group investigates multiple river processes ranging from flood protection, river restoration, to the transport of sediment, wood and plastics in rivers.

Some of our research topics include:
- Sustainable and secure water for supply, food and energy
- Adaptive and multi-functional hydraulic systems
- Resilient rivers
- Geophysical processes in rivers and lakes


## What you will find here

We use this space to share and develop the code and software that come out of our research, such as data processing tools, numerical models and analysis scripts. Repositories will be added here as projects are published.
